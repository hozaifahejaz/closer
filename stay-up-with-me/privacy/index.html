Stay Up With Me: Privacy Policy

Android app, package com.hozaifahejaz.stayupwithme. Last updated November 14, 2026.

The short version

Stay Up With Me works entirely on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never connects to the internet. We do not collect, store or share any information about you.

Who we are

Stay Up With Me is made and published by Huzaifa, an independent developer. "We" in this policy means the developer.

What stays on your phone

The app remembers which cards you have seen, your favorites, your place in each set of decks and your settings. This is saved only in the app's private storage on this device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Permissions

The app does not ask for internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or notifications. It can make the phone vibrate lightly when you tap, which you can switch off in the menu.

Sharing a question

If you tap Share, the text of that question is handed to the app you choose (for example your messaging app). That app, not Stay Up With Me, sends it, and its own privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Use "Reset my progress" in the menu to clear seen cards and saved places, and remove favorites from the Favorites screen. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything. If your phone backs up app data to your Google account, Android handles that backup under your Google settings.

Age

Stay Up With Me is meant for adults aged 18 and over. Some decks include questions about intimacy and relationships.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update and the date above will change.
