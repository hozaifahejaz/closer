Intimate questions for couples

Questions about touch, attraction, feeling wanted and feeling safe with each other.

Go at the pace that feels right for both of you. Skipping a card is always fine.

Play these with your partner

From Desire & Intimacy

All 68
  1. When do you feel most desired by me?
  2. When do you feel most at home in your body around me?
  3. What gets in the way of desire for you: stress, tiredness, distance, or something else?
  4. When has a kiss from me said more than words could?
  5. What do you need emotionally before you can feel close physically?
  6. What does a truly intimate evening look like for you, from start to finish?
  7. What's a small gesture of mine that instantly makes you feel attractive?
  8. What's the most romantic thing I've done without realising it?
  9. What turns you on about me that has nothing to do with the bedroom?
  10. Do you prefer slow and tender or spontaneous and bold, and does it change with your mood?
  11. What's a fantasy you'd feel comfortable sharing, even a small one?
  12. When do you feel most beautiful to yourself, and does what I think change that?
  13. If we ever went through a long dry spell, how would you want us to talk about it?
  14. Twenty years from now, how do you hope we still touch each other?
  15. What's your favorite physical feature of mine?
  16. In a perfect week, how often would you want us to be intimate?
  17. How much do you tell your friends about our love life?
  18. What part of our intimacy would you like us to talk about more openly?
  19. If I lost all my hair, would you still find me attractive?
  20. What's the most embarrassing thing that's ever happened to you during sex?

From Honest & Vulnerable

All 122
  1. What's the hardest thing about loving someone for you?
  2. What's a way you've tried to change for me that didn't feel true to you?
  3. Do you ever compare us to other couples, and what goes through your mind?
  4. When do I try to control things in a way that makes you feel small?
  5. What would you want me to do when you shut down?
  6. What would you tell me if you knew I'd really listen, without getting defensive?
  7. Do you wish I got a little more jealous, or a little less?
  8. Do you ever have doubts about us lasting?
  9. How do you want to be loved when you're at your worst?
  10. What part of you do you think is hardest to love?
