Funny questions for couples

Silly, strange and nostalgic questions for when you want to laugh together.

Some of them turn out to be surprisingly revealing.

Play these with your partner

All 238

  3. If we could teleport anywhere for dinner tonight, where would we go?

  5. If you could wake up tomorrow fluent in any language, which would it be?
  6. Which outfit of mine is your favorite, and why that one?
  7. What's the most thrilling thing you've ever done, and would you do it again?
  8. If cosmetic surgery were free and risk-free, would you change anything? What?

  10. If you invented a new word, what would it be and what would it mean?
  11. What movie scarred you, as a kid or as an adult?
  12. What's something that totally grosses you out that doesn't bother most people?
  13. What's the last song that got stuck in your head?
  14. Which school subjects did you love, and which did you hate?
