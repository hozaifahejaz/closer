Questions to ask before marriage

The talks worth having before you share a life: money, family, conflict, what you want the years ahead to look like.

None of these have right answers. The point is to know where each of you stands.

Play these with your partner

From Us & The Future

All 95
  1. Where do you picture us in ten years, in as much detail as you can?
  2. What does a good ordinary Tuesday look like for us in five years?
  3. What does success look like for us as a couple, not just for each of us?
  4. Where in the world could you picture us settling down, and why there?
  5. How public or private do you want our relationship to be, online and off?
  6. How will we know if we're drifting apart, and what should we do the moment we notice?
  7. What's something you hope I always remember about you, even if you change?
  8. If I got hooked on a hobby you found boring, would you join in or cheer from the sidelines?
  9. Do you think everyone should have kids, or is choosing not to just as good a life?
  10. How would you feel if I wanted to join the military?

From Money & Work

All 47
  1. What's a belief about money, work or success you inherited and still carry?
  2. Is there anything you'd love to go back and study?
  3. What's a little luxury you'd love for us to have one day?
  4. What do you think I'd need to change to love my work more?
  5. How much of who you are do you want your work to be?
  6. What's something you'd want to be able to buy without checking with me?
  7. What's the first thing you ever bought with your own money?
  8. Is there something you've been wanting to buy lately?

From Conflict & Repair

All 68
  1. What did making up look like in your family, if it happened at all?
  2. What makes an apology feel empty to you?
  3. When do you feel like you're walking on eggshells with me?
  4. How do you want us to handle disagreements in front of friends or family?
  5. What would you like me to remember about you when I'm angry with you?
  6. When have my words and my tone told you two different things?
  7. What's something you've forgiven me for that I never even knew about?
  8. If we ended up on opposite sides politically, how would we keep it from coming between us?

From Life & Meaning

All 87
  1. What do you think happens when we die, and how does that shape the way you live?
  2. Where do you look for hope when the world feels heavy?
  3. What does freedom mean to you?
  4. Do you think pain has a purpose, or is it just something we get through?
  5. What mistake do you see people make over and over that you hope we can avoid?
  6. How can you tell real love apart from infatuation?
  7. Do you think the world is getting better or worse, and why?
  8. What do you wish you understood better?

From Tiny Vows

All 3
  1. What tiny promise can you make me for this week?
  2. What vow would you make about saying sorry?
  3. Now make me promise you something. What is it?
