Questions to ask your boyfriend

Good questions for getting him talking, from easy and funny to the things he might not bring up on his own.

Ask one, listen to the whole answer, and then answer it yourself too.

Play these with your partner

From Getting Closer

All 116
  1. How do you most like to be comforted when you're having a bad day?
  2. What's a photo of us you love, and what was happening just outside the frame?
  3. What's something you're grateful for about us that you've never said out loud?
  4. Which of my quirks did you have to get used to, and which do you now defend?
  5. When you're sick, do you want to be left alone or totally fussed over?
  6. Where are we exactly alike, and where are we total opposites?
  7. How would you describe our relationship in three words?
  8. What's the best gift I could ever give you?

From Who You Are

All 148
  1. What part of yourself are you still learning to accept?
  2. What's a memory you go back to when you need courage?
  3. What did you have to figure out on your own that you wish someone had taught you?
  4. Do you think things through before you say them, or just blurt them out?
  5. What kind of art really moves you, and do you ever make any yourself?
  6. Have you ever come close to dying? What happened?
  7. What family vacations did you take as a kid?
  8. What's your favorite memory with your best friend?

From Light & Playful

All 238
  1. If money were no object, what's the most ridiculous date you'd plan for us?
  2. Which holiday or season do you love most, and what makes it yours?
  3. What's a country you'd move to just for the food?
  4. If reincarnation is real, what do you hope you come back as?
  5. Based on what you know about me, what book would you tell me to read?
  6. If you had to be an object for a week, what would you be?

  8. What's the strangest situation you've ever found yourself in?

All 122

From Us & The Future

All 95
