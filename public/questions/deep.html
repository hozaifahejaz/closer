Deep questions for couples

Questions that go past "how was your day". They are about who you are, what you are afraid of, what you believe and what you want from life.

Take them slowly. One good question can fill a whole evening, and there is no need to get through the list.

Play these with your partner

From Who You Are

All 148
  1. What belief did you hold strongly five years ago that you've since let go of?
  2. What does rest actually look like for you, not what it's supposed to look like?
  3. When have you felt the most alive?
  4. What's a small habit that makes your days go better?
  5. What's something you'd honestly feel qualified to give people advice about?
  6. When did you first feel ready for something serious and lasting?
  7. What are you most sentimental about?
  8. What's an experience you feel like you missed out on growing up or in life?

From Honest & Vulnerable

All 122
  1. Is there anything you've been wanting to say to me but haven't?
  2. What's a way you've tried to change for me that didn't feel true to you?
  3. What's a secret you once kept from someone you loved, and why?
  4. What's a question you're afraid to ask me because of what the answer might be?
  5. What's a wall you've let down with me that you've never let down for anyone else?
  6. Do you wish I got a little more jealous, or a little less?
  7. Do you think any relationship is truly affair-proof? What about ours?
  8. Is there anything you wish I'd take more initiative with?

From Life & Meaning

All 87
  1. What do you think happens when we die, and how does that shape the way you live?
  2. Where do you look for hope when the world feels heavy?
  3. What does freedom mean to you?
  4. Do you think pain has a purpose, or is it just something we get through?
  5. What mistake do you see people make over and over that you hope we can avoid?
  6. How can you tell real love apart from infatuation?
  7. Do you think the world is getting better or worse, and why?
  8. What do you wish you understood better?

From Midnight Questions

All 65
  1. Do you think we'd have found each other in another life?
  2. If our memories were a place, what would it look like?
  3. What would the ten-year-old you think of us?
  4. If you had to live your life again, would you want to remember this one?
  5. What's something you pretend not to care about but really do?
  6. Do you think you'd recognise me in a different body, in a different time?
  7. Do you believe in ghosts? What about aliens?
  8. Is there a feeling you miss, one you haven't felt in a long time?

From Us & The Future

All 95
  1. Where do you picture us in ten years, in as much detail as you can?
  2. What would you move across the world for, and what would keep you here?
  3. What's a promise you'd like us to make to each other that isn't in any vow?
  4. How should we handle it if one of us changes a lot over the next ten years?
  5. How will we know if we're drifting apart, and what should we do the moment we notice?
  6. What's one small thing you'd love us to do every day for the rest of our lives?
  7. Which couple you know seems happiest, and what would you borrow from them?
  8. If I quit my job to chase a dream, how long would you back me?
