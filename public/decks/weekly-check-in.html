Weekly Check-in: 53 weekly relationship check-in questions

A few minutes each week to stay in step. Play the Weekly Check-in deck in Duet Deck: you and your partner see the same card at the same time, on your own phones, wherever you are.

Play Weekly Check-in free
  1. From 1 to 10, how connected did you feel to us this week, and why?

  3. What was the best moment we shared this week?

  14. Did you feel physically close enough to me this week?

  50. What have you been extra sensitive about lately?
  51. What have you said no to lately that you're proud of?
