Terms of Use & Community Rules

Version 2026-10-04 · Effective October 5, 2026

Closer is a card game for private conversations between adults aged 18 or older. These rules apply to account users and guests. Before signing up, joining a guest room, or sharing answers, you must explicitly accept these terms.

Respect and consent

Invite only someone who wants to participate. You can skip questions, stop playing, leave a room, or block your partner at any time. Do not pressure someone to answer or share intimate information. Closer is not an emergency service or professional relationship, medical, or mental health advice.

Prohibited content and behaviour

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, stalking, hate speech, or discriminatory abuse.
  • No sexually explicit or pornographic content, sexual solicitation, or non-consensual sexual content.
  • No child sexual abuse or exploitation, sexual content involving minors, grooming, or trafficking. Such material and behaviour are strictly prohibited.
  • No encouragement of violence, self-harm, exploitation, or other illegal activity.
  • No impersonation, scams, spam, or disclosure of another person's private information without permission.
  • No attempts to bypass blocks, access another person's account, or interfere with the service.

Your answers and privacy

You retain ownership of your answers and allow Closer to store and show them to provide the game. Both partners see answers after both answer a card. See our Privacy Policy for data handling, retention, and account deletion. Answers are not end-to-end encrypted. Routine admin tools do not expose private answer history.

Reporting and blocking

Use Safety & support in the dashboard menu or while playing to report your partner. You choose what details to share and whether to attach a currently revealed partner answer. Only the submitted report details and selected answer are shared with authorised reviewers; your other answers are not included. Do not submit false reports or unnecessary personal information.

Blocking ends your connection. Account blocks prevent those accounts from linking again. Guest blocks apply to saved guest identities across rooms; clearing app/browser storage or using a new device creates a different identity. Guest blocking also ends the current room. Guest identities and their blocks are retained for up to one year. Account blocks remain until an associated account is deleted.

Enforcement and appeals

Reports enter a private review queue. Reviewers can close guest rooms, restrict account interactions, or dismiss a report, and record their decision. Serious safety concerns should be prioritised. We may remove content or restrict access for violations and respond to valid legal requests. To raise a safety concern or appeal an action, email hozaiphaa@gmail.com. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Accounts, changes, and availability

Keep your credentials and invite codes private. You can delete your account in the app or at Delete account. Guest progress does not transfer into accounts. Features and availability may change. Material terms changes require renewed acceptance before submitting new answers.
