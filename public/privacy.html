1. Scope and Definitions

This Policy applies to personal information processed by Closer through the Services. It does not apply to information processed by third parties that operate independently of Closer, including the operators of any websites, application stores or services that may be accessed through or in connection with the Services.

For purposes of this Policy:

  • "Personal information" means any information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • "Account" means a registered user account created with an email address and password.
  • "Couple" means two Accounts that have been linked to one another through the partner invitation feature.
  • "Guest Room" means a session created without an Account and joined by means of a room code.
  • "User Content" means the written answers, favorites and other content that you submit through the Services.
  • "Administrators" means the individuals authorized by Closer to operate and administer the Services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

When you register for and use an Account, we collect:

  • Registration information: your name (display name), email address and password. Passwords are stored exclusively in the form of a salted, one-way cryptographic hash (bcrypt) and are not retained or viewable in plain text by Closer.
  • Relationship information: a unique invitation code assigned to your Account and, where you choose to link with a partner, the identity of the linked Account.
  • User Content: the written answers you submit, together with the associated question and the date and time of submission, and the questions that you or your partner designate as favorites.
  • Communications: the content of any correspondence you send to us, including requests to exercise your privacy rights, together with the email address from which it was sent.

When you use the Services as a guest, we collect the display name you enter and any answers you submit within a Guest Room. Guest information is not associated with an Account.

2.2 Information generated through your use of the Services

  • Session information: when you sign in, we create an authentication session and record the date and time at which it was created. Only a hashed form of the session token is stored on our servers.
  • Gameplay information: the question decks you select, your progress within those decks, the current card, and, for Couples, a record of the questions already viewed.
  • Room activity information: for each active room, the room identifier, whether it is a Couple room or Guest Room, the display names of participants present, the current card, and the time of creation and most recent activity.
  • Aggregate usage metrics: counts such as the number of rooms currently open and the number of Guest Rooms created per day. These metrics do not include names, answers or other User Content.

2.3 Technical information collected automatically

As with most online services, our hosting infrastructure receives certain technical information when your device connects to the Services, including your Internet Protocol ("IP") address, browser or device type, and the date, time and address of each request. We use IP addresses and, for sign-in attempts, the submitted email address on a transient basis to enforce rate limits that protect the Services against abuse. Our hosting provider may also record operational and error logs for the purposes of diagnosing faults and maintaining the security and reliability of the Services.

2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect precise geolocation, contacts, photographs, payment card information, government identifiers, or biometric information. We do not use third-party advertising, analytics or tracking technologies, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. How We Use Information

We process personal information for the following purposes:

  1. Provision of the Services: to create and maintain Accounts, link Couples, synchronize the card displayed to both participants in real time, reveal answers once both participants have responded, and maintain answer histories, favorites and deck progress.
  2. Authentication and account management: to verify your identity when you sign in, keep you signed in on your devices, and, upon request, reset credentials or close your Account.
  3. Security and integrity: to detect, investigate and prevent fraudulent, abusive or unlawful activity, enforce rate limits, and protect the rights, property and safety of Closer, our users and others.
  4. Support: to respond to your enquiries and requests, including requests to exercise your privacy rights.
  5. Operation and improvement: to monitor the performance of the Services, diagnose technical problems, and understand how the Services are used in aggregate (for example, the number of registered Accounts or the questions most frequently answered) in order to improve them.
  6. Legal compliance: to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes and enforceable governmental requests.
Closer does not sell personal information, does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not use User Content to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable data protection law (including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation) requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on the following:

  • Performance of a contract: processing that is necessary to provide the Services you request, including Account creation, partner linking and the storage and display of User Content.
  • Legitimate interests: processing for security, abuse prevention, rate limiting, administration, troubleshooting and aggregate service improvement, where such interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligation: processing that is necessary to comply with our legal obligations.

User Content may reveal sensitive information about you, such as information concerning your relationships, beliefs, health or intimate life. You provide such information voluntarily and at your discretion. We encourage you not to submit any information you do not wish to be stored.

5. Access to Your Content

5.1 Your partner

In "Answer & reveal" mode, your answer to a card is disclosed to the other participant once both participants have submitted an answer to that card. Answers and favorites saved to a Couple are visible to both linked Accounts.

5.2 Administrators

Administrators have access to administrative tools through which they can view Account information (name, email address, registration date and partner link), Couple records, and the answers and favorites saved to Accounts. Administrators can also view information about open rooms, including Guest Rooms and the display names of participants. Administrators may use these tools to close rooms, unlink or delete Accounts, reset passwords, sign Accounts out, and delete saved answers and favorites.

Administrator access is used solely to operate, maintain and improve the Services, to provide support, to investigate and address abuse or violations of law, and to comply with legal obligations. Administrator privileges are granted only on an individual, account-by-account basis and are not assigned automatically.

Important: answers you write are not end-to-end encrypted and are accessible to Administrators as described above. If there is anything you would prefer that only your partner know, we recommend discussing it together rather than submitting it in writing.

10. Cookies and Device Storage

We use a limited number of strictly necessary storage technologies. We do not use advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies.

  • Session cookie (closer_session): a first-party, HttpOnly cookie that keeps you signed in. It is renewed while you use the Services and removed when you sign out.
  • Browser local storage: on the website, your device stores your sign-in token, the display name you last entered, and a randomly generated device identifier used to distinguish participants within a room.
  • Mobile secure storage: in the mobile applications, your sign-in token and related app data are stored on your device using the operating system's secure storage facilities (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).

Because these technologies are necessary for the Services to function, they cannot be disabled within the Services. You may clear them through your browser or device settings, which will sign you out. The Services do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals because we do not engage in the cross-site tracking to which such signals relate.

11. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse and alteration. These measures include:

  • encryption of data in transit by means of HTTPS/TLS;
  • storage of passwords and session tokens exclusively in hashed form;
  • database access restricted to the Closer server through row-level security and server-held credentials;
  • rate limiting of sign-up, sign-in, partner-linking and room-creation requests; and
  • restriction of administrative functions to individually authorized Accounts.

No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. Accordingly, while we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password and for signing out of shared devices.

Subject to applicable law, and regardless of where you reside, you may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • Rectify personal information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Delete your Account and associated personal information, including answers and favorites;
  • Receive your personal information in a portable format;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests; and
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent.

You may unlink from your partner at any time within the Services. To exercise any of the other rights listed above, please contact us using the details in Section 17. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request, typically by confirming that the request originates from the email address associated with the Account. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your rights.

Please note that deleting your Account removes your answers and the favorites of your Couple. Answers written by your partner are associated with your partner's Account and are governed by their choices.

If you believe that we have processed your personal information in violation of applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your jurisdiction. We would, however, welcome the opportunity to address your concerns first.
