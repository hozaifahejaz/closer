Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

Closer is a small app for couples to answer deep questions together. This page explains, in plain words, what we keep, why, and who can see it.

What we keep

If you create an account, we keep:

  • Your name, email and password. Your password is scrambled (hashed) before it's saved, so no one can read it, including us.
  • Your partner link: who you're linked with, plus your 6-letter invite code.
  • Your answers: the question, what you wrote, and when you wrote it.
  • Your favorites: the questions you and your partner saved.
  • Log-ins: when you signed in, so you stay signed in on your devices. Each device keeps a sign-in key in your browser's local storage and in a cookie that only Closer can read. We use no other cookies.

If you play as a guest, you don't need an account and nothing is saved to your name. The room (the current card, and any answers typed in it) exists only while it's in use. It's deleted after 6 hours without activity.

We also count how many rooms are open right now and how many guest rooms are started each day. These counts don't include names, answers or anything else about you.

How we use it

  • To run the app: showing you and your partner the same card, revealing answers once you've both answered, and keeping your history and favorites.
  • To help you: for example, setting a new password if you're locked out, or deleting your account when you ask.
  • To understand how Closer is used, through totals such as how many people signed up or which questions are answered most.

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI.

Who can see your answers

  • Your partner sees your answer to a card once you've both answered it.
  • Closer's admins (the people who run Closer) can see accounts, couples, and the answers and favorites saved to accounts. They can also see which rooms are open, including the names people entered, and can close a room or delete saved answers. They use this to run and improve the app, to help with problems, and to deal with abuse. They don't share it with anyone else.
  • If there's something you wouldn't want anyone but your partner to read, consider saying it out loud together instead of writing it.

Where it's stored

We use two services to run Closer. Your data passes through or is stored with them, and they're only allowed to use it to provide their service to us:

  • Cloudflare runs the website and the live rooms.
  • Supabase hosts the database with accounts, answers and favorites, on servers in the United States.

The page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

  • Account data (name, email, answers, favorites) stays until you delete your account or ask us to.
  • You stay signed in until you log out. Logging out deletes that sign-in and its cookie.
  • Guest rooms are deleted after 6 hours without activity.
  • Cloudflare holds a working copy of each couple's room: the decks you chose and where you are in them. It's cleared after a year without activity; your answers and favorites stay in your account.

Your choices

  • Unlink from your partner at any time in the app. Answers you already saved together stay unless you ask us to delete them.
  • See, correct or delete your data: email us and we'll send you a copy, fix it, or delete your account. Deleting an account removes your answers and your couple's favorites.

Age

Closer is meant for adults (18 and over). We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, the site only works over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and the database can only be reached through the Closer server. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we keep or who can see it, we'll say so in the app.
